NLP & Date Resolver Engine

Natural language inputs often contain complex relative dates, day offsets, or combined filter preferences. The DateResolver and LocalRuleAiServiceImpl components provide deterministic resolution for temporal and preference queries.

Date Expression Parsing

The DateResolver uses the app reference date (Sep 7, 2026) as an anchor to calculate exact DateTime preferences:

User Prompt Resolved Date Type Resolved Output Date(s)
"tomorrow" Exact Date Sep 8, 2026
"next weekend" Date Range Sep 12, 2026 – Sep 13, 2026
"next week" Date Range Sep 14, 2026 – Sep 20, 2026
"next month 7" / "7th of next month" Exact Date Oct 7, 2026
"next weekend after the 12th" Date Range Offset Sep 19, 2026 – Sep 20, 2026
"flexible" / "anytime" Flexible Any valid date range

Bidirectional Layover, Duration & Price Preferences

The rule engine evaluates intent in both directions (lesser vs. higher / cheap vs. expensive) and updates SearchCriteria accordingly:

Phrases Matched Sort Preference Applied Constraint Set
"lesser layover", "shorter duration", "min layover" SortPreference.fastest Ranks fastest / shortest total duration first
"higher layover", "more duration", "longer layover" SortPreference.longest Ranks longer journeys first
"cheapest", "lowest fare", "under 20k" SortPreference.cheapest Max price capped at parsed amount
"expensive", "premium", "costliest" SortPreference.expensive Ranks premium fares first
"direct", "non-stop", "no layover" Unchanged directOnly = true
